# Instagram collection

## Access path

Check already available permitted connectors/export interfaces first. Verify current capabilities before relying on them. When browser access is appropriate, use the authorized browser tool and current documented API; preserve user's login session and obey tool/URL policy. Search engines and official sites identify handles/URLs, not authoritative current counters. Avoid promising a scraper or download always works.

Preflight a small varied sample: profile identity/followers, chronological grid counts, publication timestamp, one candidate detail/comments, actual media and audio. Save a capability report. An access failure can limit the report; never claim inaccessible fields were reviewed. Temporary failure: one retry after refreshing observed state, then another authorized access path if available. Policy/authentication denial: no bypass. Log status and continue independent available accounts.

## Browser workflow

One owner per controlled tab. Use short page batches; save extracted records immediately outside volatile browser memory. Prefer compact accessibility/DOM output and observed media asset manifests. Never inspect hidden account state, credentials or unrelated DMs. Do not collect signed asset URLs in public reports.

Grid: handle, follower count, source URL, observed time, chronological position, pin/out-of-order flag, Reel ID and displayed views. Open enough detail pages to establish dates throughout claimed coverage, not only top counters. A screenshot hover can remove a visible view count; record missing rather than guess.

Detail: canonical Reel ID, publication owner/coauthors, timestamp of publication, edited timestamp separately, caption, duration, visible reaction counters and surface labels. The first `time` element may be an edit or comment timestamp; verify its UI role. Store the original timestamp/timezone and source evidence. Facebook counters stay separate. A public repost count must not be relabeled as sends/shares or saves.

Comments: visible count and total counter separately, original URL where available, quoted fragment or faithful paraphrase, likes and whether it is a reply. Sample both top and recent comments if supported; record sort/coverage. Label product questions, availability/fit objections, intent, admiration for person, giveaway tags, requested keywords, brand replies and spam. Do not call the visible subset exhaustive or treat all engagement as purchase intent.

Media: verify ID/duration/first and last scene against the actual page before analysis. Use observed authorized media assets or permitted video tools, not unrelated resource guesses. Save one adequate variant, check stream/duration, cache by Reel ID/media identity. Follow video-review.md for audio and frame limitations. Reuse cached media for daily counter refreshes.

Audio: capture card URL/ID/name and the timestamped count of uses if exposed. Original-audio attribution is not automatically the music recording's identity. Compare repeated observations of the same ID; counts from different regional/platform surfaces may not be comparable. TikTok Creative Center does not establish Instagram audio momentum. Check track availability in the publishing account when preparing a post, not by assuming rights/access from competitor use.

## Daily refresh

Read approved registry and due queue. Browse newest posts in each approved accessible profile until the seven-day boundary or declared cap. Register eligible new posts with verified timestamps; late discoveries have missing prior days. Refresh eligible known Reel counters and linked audio cards once daily, deduplicating coauthored copies by canonical ID. Update comments in depth on selection, major change or final review, not every day without purpose. No per-post polling after 168 hours. Unavailable profiles remain in the coverage log; retry unresolved identity only on new evidence or a bounded periodic identity review.
