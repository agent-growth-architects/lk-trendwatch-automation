# TikTok collection

Use currently available permitted connectors, exports or browser tools. Verify any API/CLI against current official documentation before installing or promising behavior. TikTokApi or yt-dlp may fail; do not claim guaranteed access. Preserve timestamps, source surface and actual metric names.

Collect sequential publication metadata to the declared boundary/cap, account followers, caption, duration, sound ID/title, public counters and a documented comments sample. Distinguish official brand posts, explicit placements, suspected affiliations and general creator content. Keywords can find placements but absence of two placements is not proof of irrelevance; visual product evidence may matter.

Creative Center can supply TikTok-specific trend context with geo/time/source. Do not transfer it to Instagram without checking Instagram evidence. Apply method.md and video-review.md, use the same seven-day observation rules. Preserve public/private metric boundaries and do not infer watch-through from engagement.
